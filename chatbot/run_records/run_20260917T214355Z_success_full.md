### 6. Recommended curriculum structure

1. **Core Module (All tracks) – “Agentic AI Foundations”**  
   * Topics: AutoGen, CrewAI, LangGraph, LLM‑as‑a‑Judge, prompt‑engineering, tool‑use APIs, evaluation metrics, responsible AI.  
   * Hands‑on labs: Build a simple autonomous agent that scrapes data, makes a decision, and logs audit trails.  

2. **Sector‑specific Advanced Modules**  
   * **Finance Track** – “Autonomous Financial Systems”  
     - Deep dive into Azure AI Services, Agentic Frameworks, and regulatory AI.  
     - Lab: Construct an autonomous portfolio‑rebalancing agent with compliance checks.  
   * **Healthcare Track** – “Autonomous Clinical Decision Support”  
     - Focus on LLM‑as‑a‑Judge for clinical protocol validation, Conversational AI for patient triage, and privacy‑preserving multi‑agent pipelines.  
     - Lab: Build a triage chatbot that coordinates with a treatment‑plan generator and logs HIPAA‑compliant audit trails.  

3. **Capstone Project (cross‑track)**  
   * Students partner with industry (bank, fintech, hospital, health‑tech) to design, prototype, and evaluate a real‑world agentic AI system aligned with sector regulations.  

---

### 7. Trade‑off / Caveat

*Adding sector‑specific depth will lengthen programme length or increase elective load.* Teams must decide whether to extend the master’s duration, replace less‑used electives, or offer the sector modules as **specializations** that students can select after completing the common core.
