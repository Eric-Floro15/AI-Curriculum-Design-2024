### 5. Cluster Context: Where Agentic AI Lives

The cluster overview confirms agentic AI straddles two focused clusters:

- **Cluster 1 — Machine Learning & Generative AI (174 skills)** — the semantic home for LLMs, generative AI, NLP, transformers, and model-level skills. The top cluster for agentic AI's core.
- **Cluster 2 — Cloud, DevOps & AI Systems Deployment (282 skills)** — contributes the deployment half: containerisation, APIs, observability, Kubernetes. Relevant but not the primary home.
- **Cluster 3 — Data Engineering & Data Platforms (159 skills)** — contributes Python, SQL, distributed systems; lower lift for agentic AI specifically.
- **Cluster 7 — Data Science & Statistical Foundations (69 skills)** — lower overlap with agentic AI; Scikit-Learn (lift 3.97×, z=2.28) and Reinforcement Learning (4.54×, z=2.15) are the links.

The key finding: agentic AI **bridges Clusters 1 and 2 in a specific way** — it uses Cluster 1's generative models as cognitive engines and Cluster 2's deployment infrastructure as the runtime, but its *distinctive* demand (orchestration, multi-agent, RAG, vector retrieval, LLMOps) is not well captured by either cluster's generic course. This further supports a standalone course that bridges both without duplicating either.

### Trade-Off and Caveats

**The main trade-off is toolchain velocity vs. foundational depth.** The highest-lift skills are framework-level (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen) — these are fast-moving and may look different in two years. A curriculum that teaches the *conceptual pattern* (tool-augmented reasoning loops, memory management, agent evaluation) and uses frameworks as *implementations* of those patterns will age better than one organised around specific library APIs. The responsible-AI and observability signals (both clearing z ≥ 2) suggest evaluation and governance should be first-class concerns, not afterthoughts.

**Second trade-off: the link to RL.** Queen's has paired "Reinforcement Learning and Agentic AI" in a single course. The data supports this: Reinforcement Learning appears at lift 4.54×, z=2.15 — distinctively co-demanded alongside agentic AI. Pairing RL foundations (value functions, reward shaping, RLHF) with agentic systems architecture is a coherent design that also reduces course proliferation.

**Data caveat:** the lift table reflects job postings as of Winter 2026. Given the pace of development — evidenced by the VAKRA benchmark paper, the MAST enterprise-failure research, and the rapid adoption figures in the Coursera report — the specific tooling signals (which framework is highest-lift) may shift within 12–18 months. The structural signals (RAG, multi-agent orchestration, evaluation, responsible AI) are more durable and should anchor the course design.
