#### 2. Advanced Information Extraction & Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

### Critical Trade-off & Caveat

The professor must carefully navigate the **trade-off between execution autonomy and systemic risk**. Allowing agents to autonomously execute financial trades or make real-time portfolio decisions (high autonomy) maximizes efficiency and exploits market opportunities. However, as highlighted by the *MIT Technology Review AI* and the development of benchmarks like *IT-Bench/MAST*, multi-agent systems in complex environments are prone to cascading, non-deterministic failures and tool-calling errors.

**Recommendation:** The curriculum should strongly enforce a **"Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL)** architecture for any action involving capital movement, treating fully autonomous agents as "drafting" or "advising" systems, while reserving final transaction execution for humans until safety and alignment frameworks reach high reliability.
